# FunkoDex — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 September 2026**

FunkoDex is made by Celtic Heart Steamworks. This policy explains exactly what
the app does and does not do with your information. It is short because the app
collects very little.

## The short version

**Your collection never leaves your phone.** What you own, what you paid,
condition, notes, acquisition dates and your photographs are stored only on your
device. They are not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not shared with anyone.
We operate no account system and no user database.

## What stays on your device

- Items you own, and whether you own them
- Price paid, condition, acquisition date, personal notes
- Photographs you take of your items
- App settings and preferences

This data is written to a local database on your phone. If you use **Backup**,
the file is saved to your own Downloads folder and goes nowhere else. Uninstall
the app and this data is gone unless you made a backup yourself.

## What the app sends, and when

### Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode the app may send **the barcode number** to third-party
product databases to identify the item. No information about you is attached.
These services have their own privacy policies:

- PriceCharting — pricing and product data
- UPCitemdb — barcode lookup

### Community catalog contributions — off by default

If you turn on **"Contribute to community database"** in Settings, the app
shares corrections and additions you make to *product* information so other
users benefit. This is off unless you switch it on, and can be switched off at
any time.

A contribution contains **only facts about the product**:

barcode, product name, franchise, category, pop number, suggested retail price,
image URL, and whether the item is a chase, exclusive or vaulted edition.

A contribution **never** contains: whether you own the item, what you paid,
condition, your notes, your photographs, your name, your email address, your
location, or any device or advertising identifier.

Contributions travel to a server we operate, which validates them and records
them in a public repository at
<https://github.com/celticht32/funko-upc-community>. That repository holds
product data only.

**One technical identifier:** each install generates a random identifier used
solely to limit how many contributions one device can send per day. It is not
linked to you, is not stored in the public repository, and is discarded from our
logs. Reinstalling the app generates a new one.

### Catalog updates

The app periodically downloads the product catalog from the public repository
above. This is a plain file download. Nothing about you is sent in order to
receive it.

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes, while the scanner screen is open.
Images from the camera are processed on your device and are not transmitted or
stored. Photographs you deliberately take of your items are saved on your device
only, as described above.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are used only for price alerts you set up
yourself. They are generated on your device.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect personal information
- We do not track you across apps or websites
- We do not show advertising
- We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
- We have no accounts, so there is nothing to log in to and nothing to breach

## Children

FunkoDex is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them. Since the app collects no personal information from
anyone, there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

## Your control

- **Stop contributing** — turn the Settings switch off. Nothing further is sent.
- **Delete everything** — uninstall the app. All collection data is removed.
- **Already-published contributions** — because contributions carry no
  identifier tying them to you, we cannot locate yours after the fact. They are
  product facts, not personal data. If you believe something you contributed
  should be corrected or removed from the public catalog, contact us and we will
  correct the record.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here
with a new date.
